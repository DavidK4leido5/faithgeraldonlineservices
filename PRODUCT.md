## Platform

web

## Users

Primary customers are individuals in the Philippines who need help completing
everyday digital money transactions: sending money, paying bills, converting
cash to e-wallet balance and back, and handling supported account or
credit-related transactions they cannot complete on their own.

Two confirmed situations, both first-class:

- **Local customers** near Himamaylan City, Negros Occidental and Silang, Cavite
  who want a real person they can meet and transact with face to face.
- **Online customers anywhere in the Philippines** who will never meet in person
  and decide to trust the business purely on response speed, clarity, and proof.

The job they are hiring for is not "a service list". It is: *help me finish this
transaction correctly, and let me see that it actually happened.*

## Product Purpose

Faith Gerald Online Services assists customers through supported online
transactions and payments that are difficult, slow, or unclear to complete
alone. It exists to make those transactions simple, fast, and understandable,
with clear communication from first message to completion.

Success means a customer understands the process, the requirements, and the
fees before sending anything, and completes the transaction without confusion
or a dispute.

## Positioning

The differentiator is **staying with the customer during the transaction**,
including video-call assistance when additional communication or verification is
needed. Most competing online helpers go silent after payment and leave the
customer waiting. This business stays reachable and visible through the process,
so the customer can follow what is happening rather than waiting on faith.

Local meet-up availability in Himamaylan City and Silang, Cavite supports this
by giving local customers a verifiable in-person anchor. Meet-up is an
availability, not a permanent branch or office.

## Operating Context

- Most transactions are arranged and completed online through messaging.
- Video-call assistance is offered when extra communication or verification is
  needed.
- Meet-up arrangements exist for local customers but must be confirmed in
  advance; availability and schedule change.
- Fees, requirements, and the process are explained before the customer
  proceeds.
- Platform availability for any specific transaction changes over time.

remittance, authorized account assistance, safe handling, and video-call
assistance during the process.

Confirmed supported transaction assistance (explicitly *may include*, not
guaranteed): SPayLater, TikTok PayLater, LazPayLater, GGives, GCredit, Home
Credit Quarta, unverified ShopeePay, unverified PayMaya, credit card limit,

## Product Principles

1. **Never overclaim.** A smaller true claim beats a bigger false one. Where
   availability is uncertain, say so in the same breath as the offer.
2. **Explain before you transact.** Fees, requirements, and steps are stated
   before the customer commits anything.
3. **Stay present.** Availability during the transaction is the product, not
   just support after it.
4. **Proof over promises.** Show completed transactions with real images
   instead of adjectives.
5. **Protect the customer's information.** Receipt images are shown as images;
   their sensitive contents are never repeated as page text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The audience is broad, mobile-first, and includes customers on low-end Android
devices and slower connections. Requirements that follow:

- Readable at 320px and up, with no horizontal scrolling.
- Touch targets sized for comfortable tapping.
- Body text meeting WCAG AA contrast.
- All content present as real HTML text so it is readable, searchable, and
  usable by assistive technology.
- Full keyboard operation and visible focus states.
- Motion reduced or removed under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.
