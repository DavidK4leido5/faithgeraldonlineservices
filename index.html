FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our services, availability, and how a transaction is processed.

What services does Faith Gerald Online Services offer?

Faith Gerald Online Services assists with cash-in and cash-out, e-wallet transactions, bills payment, remittance, and supported account-related transactions. You can also request video-call assistance during the process. Available services may change depending on current availability.

How do I start a transaction?

Send us a message and tell us which service you need. We will explain the available process, requirements, and any applicable fees before you proceed.

Do you provide cash-in and cash-out assistance?

Yes. We assist with cash-in and cash-out transactions through supported platforms and channels. Availability may depend on the platform, the amount, and current service availability, so please confirm with us first.

Can you assist with bills payment?

Yes. We assist with payments for supported utilities, bills, and internet services. Send us the bill details and we will confirm whether the biller and payment channel are supported before you pay.

Can I ask for assistance through video call?

Yes. Video-call assistance is available when additional communication or verification is needed, so you can follow the process and confirm the details as it happens.

Where are your services available?

We serve customers in Himamaylan City, Negros Occidental and Silang, Cavite, and we also handle transactions online. Meet-up arrangements are not a permanent branch or office, so availability, location, and schedule must be confirmed with us before a transaction.

How do I verify the details before sending money?

We explain the process, requirements, and applicable fees first, and we confirm the final amount and recipient details with you before anything is sent. If any detail is unclear, ask us before you proceed.

Are all transactions available at all times?

No. Availability may depend on the specific transaction, the platform or account involved, the requirements of that transaction, and current availability. We will tell you honestly if something cannot be processed at that time.
